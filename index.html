        Questions? We've got you.

        Who should use this hub?

        Prospective students and families can explore BRYC, express interest, and—after launch—apply for the Fellowship.

        Can I apply from my phone?

        The public experience is being designed mobile-first.

        What grades are eligible?

        Eligibility will be displayed using the approved rules for the applicable program year, with grade-specific application logic. Contact RAM if you're unsure.
